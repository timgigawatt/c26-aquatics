Ashley Wilson brings a lifelong love of swimming, fitness, and movement — and of helping others feel strong and confident. She studied Kinesiology at California State University, San Bernardino, where she taught group exercise classes and worked as a swim instructor.

She especially enjoys helping swimmers build confidence in the water, improve their technique, and become stronger, more comfortable swimmers. She knows every swimmer learns at a different pace and loves creating a positive environment where swimmers feel encouraged to try, improve, and celebrate their progress. Her fitness background also includes strength training, distance running, cheerleading, and coaching, and she has completed multiple half marathons.

Whether she's helping a new swimmer get comfortable in the water or encouraging someone through a challenging workout, Ashley brings energy, patience, and positivity to every session — so every person leaves feeling stronger, more capable, and more confident. Outside of C26, she's a mom of two, a real estate professional, and a business owner.
