Illia Kosenko is a dedicated swimming coach with nine years of experience as a competitive swimmer and a strong background in professional swimming. His years as an athlete gave him a deep understanding of technique, discipline, endurance, and the mindset it takes to succeed in the water.

He holds a degree in Physical Education and brings five years of coaching experience with swimmers of all ages and skill levels — from young children just beginning their journey to experienced athletes sharpening their technique and performance. His approach is built around safety, confidence, personalized instruction, and continuous improvement, and he loves helping swimmers develop strong fundamentals, reach their goals, and build a lifelong love for the sport.
